# Roadmap

Waves as in the check catalogue (`docs/spec/pruefkatalog.md`):

- **Wave 1–2:** passive checks, browser rendering, privacy hints (done)
- **Wave 3:** active probes after proof of ownership (in progress), accessibility hints (axe-core)
- **Wave 4:** LLM endpoints (open edge functions, cost guard), auth rate limits
- **Wave 5:** checks behind the login with two test accounts

Ideas and votes: [Discussions](https://github.com/muka-dev/edeltraud/discussions).
