# FAQ

### Does a green light mean my app is secure?
No. It means the checks that ran found nothing. Everything behind the login and all
business logic still need a human review. There is no certificate and no guarantee.

### Does the scanner attack my app?
The passive plans only read what the app delivers publicly. Active probes run only after
the owner proves control of the domain, and they only read.

### Can I scan apps of other people?
Only with the permission of the operator. Operators who do not want scans can ask the
operator of an instance to add their domain to `BLOCKED_DOMAINS`.

### What is not open source?
The ready-made fix prompts, the designed report, payment and the WatchDog of the hosted
service at [scan.m-ko.de](https://scan.m-ko.de).

### Why AGPL-3.0?
Anyone may run, study and improve the scanner. Whoever offers a modified version as a
network service shares the changes under the same licence.
